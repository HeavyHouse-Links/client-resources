TRAINERIZE SETUP GUIDE

Everything you need to get set up in the app, step by step. Five minutes and you are ready to train.

STEP 1

Find your invite email

Check the inbox of the email address you gave me for an invitation from Heavy House via Trainerize. Not there? Check your spam or junk folder, it hides there more often than not. Still nothing? Message me and I will resend it.

STEP 2

Accept the invite and set your password

Open the email and tap the button inside. You will be asked to create a password, pick one you will remember, this is your login for the app.

STEP 3

Download the app

Grab the Trainerize app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). It is free.

STEP 4

Log in

Open the app and log in with your email address and the password you just created.

STEP 5

Turn notifications on

When the app asks, allow notifications. This is how you get session reminders and it genuinely makes the difference between a logged week and a forgotten one.

STEP 6

Find your program

Your training lives in the Calendar tab: every scheduled session, cardio and check-in item sits on its day. Tap today to see what is on.

STEP 7

Log a workout

Open the session, and as you train, enter the weight and repetitions for every set, then tick the set done. Log every set, every session. This data is how I build your next block, without it I am guessing.

STEP 8

Weigh-ins and photos

When a weigh-in or progress photo appears on your calendar, tap it and enter your number or upload your photos right there in the app. Fasted, first thing, before any food or liquid. The full how-to lives in the Progress Photo Guide back in the hub.

One thing the app is NOT for: messaging. All communication happens on WhatsApp, not through the app. Questions, problems, life getting in the way, send them straight to my number. The app is for logging, WhatsApp is for talking.
