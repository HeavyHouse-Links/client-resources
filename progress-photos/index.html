PROGRESS PHOTO GUIDE

Consistent photos are the clearest way to see progress the scale can't show. Follow this every time.
WHEN

Every Saturday morning, fasted — before any food or liquids.

Same day, same time, every week. This keeps water weight, food volume, and bloating consistent between photos so what you're comparing is real change, not noise.

HOW TO POSE

Three photos every time: front, side, back. Use the templates below as your guide.

Template — Men
Template — Women
TIPS FOR CONSISTENCY
  • Same spot, same background every week — makes side-by-side comparisons accurate.
  • Phone at chest height, held straight — not tilted up or down, which distorts your shape.
  • Same distance from the camera each time.
  • Consistent lighting — natural light near a window works well. Avoid direct flash.
  • Minimal, form-fitting clothing — shorts/underwear and sports bra work best so changes are visible.
  • Stand relaxed and natural — don't flex or suck in. You want an honest baseline, not a flattering angle.
