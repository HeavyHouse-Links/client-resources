EATING OUT & ON THE GO

Life happens. You don't need to avoid social situations because of your nutrition plan — you just need a plan for them.

One meal won't derail your progress. Consistent effort over weeks is what drives results, not any single meal.

BEFORE YOU GO
  • Got something coming up? Message me in advance and we'll work around it — don't just wing it.
  • If you can, check the menu online beforehand and pick out 3–5 options you'd genuinely be happy with. Send them through to me on WhatsApp and we'll land on the best one together — so you're not missing out, just making the smartest call available.
  • Don't starve yourself all day to "save up" calories for the meal — it usually backfires into overeating.
AT THE RESTAURANT
  • Go protein-forward — grilled, baked, or roasted over fried or crumbed.
  • Ask for sauces and dressings on the side, so you control how much goes on.
  • Swap fries or extra carbohydrates for salad or vegetables where it's an option.
  • Alcohol counts as calories too — it's fine in moderation, just factor it in rather than pretending it doesn't exist.
FAST FOOD / ON THE GO
  • Same rules apply — protein-forward options over fried where you can.
  • Watch liquid calories — stick to sugar-free drinks like Coke Zero, Pepsi Max, or Diet Coke instead of regular soft drinks, juices, or frappe-style drinks.
  • Something is almost always better than skipping meals entirely and arriving starving at your next one.

AFTERWARDS

Get back on track with your very next meal. Don't feel guilty, and don't try to "make up for it" with extreme restriction the next day — that's how a good week turns into a bad one. Make the best choice available, enjoy it, and move on.
