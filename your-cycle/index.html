TRAINING & YOUR CYCLE

Your cycle affects training, appetite, sleep and the scale. Here's what's actually happening — and how we work with it, not against it.
Nothing on this page is a rule. Every cycle is different, and some months won't follow any pattern at all. This is a map, not a schedule — your plan stays built around you.
THE SCALE LIES (SOMETIMES)
Water retention moves the scale around your period — on average about 0.5kg, and for some women 2kg or more, usually peaking in the days before and settling as your period ends. That number is water, not fat. Nothing you did wrong, nothing to fix.
This is exactly why we judge trends, not days. Weekly and monthly direction is the truth — a single weigh-in never is.
Scale jumped and your period is due? Log it, roll your eyes at it, let me know and move on.
WHAT YOU MIGHT NOTICE
On your period: energy can dip, especially the first day or two. Training is still safe and often helps — but an easier session is a smart session, not a soft one.
The week or two after: some women feel their strongest here — energy up, lifts feel better. The research is genuinely mixed on how much is physiology versus individual pattern, which is exactly why tracking your own months beats any textbook.
The week before your period: appetite up, sleep patchier, energy flatter, everything feels heavier. Completely normal, completely hormonal — and it passes.
None of this is guaranteed. Let's work together to track how YOU actually feel across a few months and your own pattern will show itself.
HOW WE TRAIN AROUND IT
The program doesn't change automatically — but how you feel is data I want. Note it when you log sessions and in your check-ins.
A flat week before your period is expected, not backsliding. The strength isn't gone — it shows back up.
If a session feels impossible rather than just hard, message me — we adjust the week, not abandon it.
Consistency across the whole month beats hero sessions in a good week.
APPETITE & CRAVINGS
Cravings before your period are physiology, not weak willpower. Research shows appetite genuinely rises in that week — and your body also burns slightly more at rest, so the hunger has a reason.
Plan for them instead of trying to tough it out. The Low-Cal Treats guide on the hub exists for exactly this week.
Protein and sleep are your best blunting tools — both take the edge off the hunger swings.
Never "make up" for a big day by starving the next one — that turns one hormonal day into a bad week.
WHEN TO TALK TO ME — OR A DOCTOR
If your period becomes irregular or stops while training hard and dieting — tell me straight away. That's an important signal, and it usually means we adjust your plan, not push harder.
Severe pain, very heavy bleeding, or anything that doesn't feel right for you — that's a doctor conversation, and worth having.
Starting or changing contraception? Flag it. The research says most pills don't consistently change weight, but the injection can, and any change can shift appetite, fluid and energy for a while — I'd rather adjust expectations than misread your data.
I'm a coach, not a medical professional. I will always defer to your doctor — my job is making sure your plan works with your body, not against it.
Your cycle isn't an obstacle — it's information. Track it, tell me how the weeks actually feel, and we'll use it to make the plan fit you better than any generic program ever could.
