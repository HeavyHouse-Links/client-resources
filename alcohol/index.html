PLAY IT SMART
Pick your number before you leave the house. Deciding at the bar after three drinks never goes well. Three or four drinks planned beats six unplanned.
Make spirit + zero-sugar mixer your default order. It turns every drink into roughly 70 calories and nobody at the table knows or cares what's in your glass.
One water between drinks. Slows the pace, halves the drinks, and tomorrow-you will thank you at training.
Eat a proper meal before you go out. Protein and carbohydrates. Skipping dinner to "save calories" just means you drink faster on an empty stomach and order the worst thing on the late-night menu.
The kebab does more damage than the drinks. Four vodka sodas is under 300 calories — the 1am feed can be 1,000+. If you win the end of the night, you've won the night.
THE MORNING AFTER

Quick science, no lecture: alcohol dehydrates you, blocks the deep sleep your body repairs muscle in, and puts recovery on pause while it's being processed. You can't skip that bill — but you can decide how the day after goes. Here's how:

Water before coffee. Most of what you're feeling is dehydration. A big glass of water (add electrolytes if it was a big one) does more than the coffee will — then have the coffee.
Eat normal meals, starting with protein. Don't fast to "make up for it" — that's how a rough morning turns into a 3pm binge. A proper protein-heavy breakfast steadies everything.
Move, don't punish. A walk or an easy session helps you feel human again. Punishment cardio doesn't burn off last night — it just teaches you that nights out end in punishment, and that mindset never lasts.
Expect training to feel flat for a day or two. Alcohol wrecks the quality of your sleep even if you got plenty of it, and your body notices. Don't schedule your biggest session of the week for the morning after — plan around it instead of fighting it.
Back on plan at the next meal — not next Monday. One night out never broke anyone's progress. The write-off weekend that follows is what does the damage. Next meal, back to normal, done.
