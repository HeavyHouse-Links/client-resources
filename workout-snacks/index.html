Fuel going in, repair coming out. Before training it's quick carbohydrates and not much else; after training it's protein. Every item is a sealed single serve, and every one links straight to the product at Woolworths.
BEFORE TRAINING

Eat 30–90 minutes out. Carbohydrates are the fuel — keep fat and fibre low so it digests fast and sits light.

AFTER TRAINING

The 30-minute "anabolic window" is mostly myth — your total daily protein matters far more than the sprint to the shaker. But finishing a session is a great trigger to bank a serve: aim for 20g+ within a couple of hours.
