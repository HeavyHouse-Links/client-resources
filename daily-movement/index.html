DAILY MOVEMENT

Your training is a few hours a week. The other 160-odd hours decide most of your result — and the biggest lever in them is simply how much you move.
WHY IT MATTERS
The movement outside the gym burns more than the gym. Across a week, walking, standing, stairs and general moving about typically burn more total energy than your training sessions do. It's the quiet engine of every fat loss result.
Steps beat punishment cardio. Walking doesn't wreck recovery, doesn't spike your appetite the way hard cardio does, and you can do it every single day without needing anything back. That's why it works — it repeats.
It's also recovery. Easy movement gets blood to sore muscles and beats a couch day between sessions — movement is medicine, same as the Recovery guide says.
THE TARGET
Start from where you are, not from Instagram. Check your phone's average for the last month, then add 2,000 to it. That's your target. Someone averaging 4,000 doesn't need 10,000 overnight — they need 6,000 consistently.
8,000–10,000 most days is the long-term zone. Not a magic number — just the range where most people's results live. Your plan may set something specific; that wins.
Measure it the same way every day. Same phone, same pocket, or the same watch. Consistency of the measure matters more than its accuracy — you're tracking the trend, not auditing every step.
MAKING IT FIT
Walk after meals. Ten to fifteen minutes after two meals a day quietly covers most of the target, helps digestion, and steadies energy through the afternoon.
Stack it onto things you already do. Phone calls walking, park at the far end, stairs by default, get off one stop early. None of it needs willpower once it's the default.
Shift workers and FIFO: watch the home weeks. On site or on shift your steps are often huge, then they crash on days off. The step target matters most on the quiet days — that's when the couch does its damage.
Weather's rubbish? Move anyway. Shopping centre laps, a treadmill on incline with a podcast, pacing during the footy. The target doesn't care where the steps happen.
